test(e2e): use toHaveCSS for box-shadow focus ring assertions

Replaces the two `getComputedStyle().boxShadow` reads followed by
`expect(string).toMatch(...)` in tests/browser/popup_a11y.spec.ts with
Playwright's auto-retrying `await expect(locator).toHaveCSS("box-shadow",
regex)`.

Why: `.btn` and `.detection-item` in extension/popup.html declare
`transition: all 0.2s`. The synchronous read in `getComputedStyle` (called
via `locator.evaluate`) captures the box-shadow MID-TRANSITION — often
`rgba(0,0,0,0)` — and the regex race-fails the test in headless Chromium.
Playwright's `toHaveCSS` polls through the 0.2s transition and is the
idiomatic Playwright replacement.

Deliberately carved out of PR #494 / commit 1b323e9 to keep that PR
atomic on the `mockChromeAPI` fix. Applied on chore/lint-e2e-transitions
so the dedicated E2E Lint job on this branch can trip clean post-merge;
the same commit is also opened as a standalone followup PR (#497) carrying
only this rewrite, in keeping with PR #494's atomicity choice.
